Why We Exist

Born From a Glass of Cloudy Water

Crystalina started the way most good New York stories do — with a problem in an old building. After one too many rust-tinted glasses following a water main break, our founder went looking for a filter actually designed for city conditions: lead-era plumbing, chloramine-heavy treatment, and apartments where you can't touch the pipes.

It didn't exist. So we built it. Every Crystalina system is specced against what actually shows up in New York City water — and backed by a team that lives here, drinks it, and answers the phone when you call.
