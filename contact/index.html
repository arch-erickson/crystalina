Questions we are asked

Before you write, the short answers

How quickly can a system be fitted?
Most systems fit in under an hour with household tools, and the manual for each one is in Manuals and Guides. If you would rather not, our own team fits it for you anywhere in the five boroughs; ask for installation when you order or write to us.
Is delivery really free?
In the five boroughs, on any order over $99, yes. Below that the delivery charge is shown at checkout before you pay anything.
What if it is not right for my home?
Sixty days to return it, and a one-year warranty on every system. Write to us with the order number and we arrange the rest.
I rent. Can I still have filtered water?
Yes. A countertop system connects to the tap without touching the plumbing and leaves with you when you move. The quiz will point you to one.
What does a reverse osmosis system take out?
Lead, chlorine and chloramine, PFAS, microplastics, rust and sediment, and fluoride, at the rates shown on each product page. The figures come from laboratory conditions and vary with your building's pressure and plumbing.
How often do filters need changing, and will I remember?
It depends on the stage and on your water, typically every six to twelve months. We keep count: when a filter is due we say so, and the replacement arrives at your door.
Which system should I choose?
Five questions in Find Your Filter match you to a tier. Or call us: we would rather talk through your building than sell you the wrong box.
Where are the manuals?
In Manuals and Guides, one per product, to read in the page or to keep as a file.
