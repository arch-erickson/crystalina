Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and how to have it removed.

Last updated 2 November 2026.

Who we are

Crystalina sells and installs home water filtration systems in New York City. You can reach us at info@crystalina.org or on (917) 809-4803.

What we collect

Only what we need to sell you a filter and support it afterwards.

  • When you order: your name, email address, phone number and delivery address, and what you bought.
  • When you create an account: your name and email address. If you sign in with Google, Google tells us your name and email address and nothing else. We never see or store a password.
  • When you contact us: your name, email address and your message.
  • When you join the newsletter: your email address.

We do not collect anything about you that you have not typed in yourself.

Cookies and tracking

We use two pieces of browser storage, both necessary for the site to work: one that keeps you signed in, and one that remembers what is in your cart. There is no advertising tracking on this site, and no analytics that identify you.

Who else handles your information

We use a small number of services to run the shop. Each one sees only what it needs.

  • Supabase stores the database behind the shop, including your account and your orders.
  • Cloudflare serves the website itself.
  • Google handles sign in, if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Resend sends order confirmations and replies to your messages.
  • Stripe handles payment. Your card details go directly to Stripe and never reach us.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone for advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as tax and warranty obligations require. Everything else, including newsletter subscriptions and contact messages, we keep until you ask us to remove it.

Your choices

Email info@crystalina.org and we will tell you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Every newsletter has an unsubscribe link. Deleting your account does not delete order records we are required to keep.

Children

This shop is not intended for children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy we will change the date at the top. If the change is significant, we will say so by email to anyone with an account.
