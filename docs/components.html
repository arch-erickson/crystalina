Header and menu

The header at the top of this page is the component. Its data is NAV_LINKS and CATEGORIES in js/main.js. Hover or focus Shop to open the menu; on a touch screen the first tap opens it and the second follows the link. On the home page the same header starts transparent over the hero and turns white on scroll.

The announce bar, the phone link, the centred lockup, the actions and the navigation row are all one renderHeader(). Nothing in a page head duplicates it.
