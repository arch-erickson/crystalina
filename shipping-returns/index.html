Shipping and Returns

Where we deliver, what it costs, and what to do if something is wrong.

Last updated 2 November 2026.

Where we deliver

All five boroughs of New York City: Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, the Bronx and Staten Island. We do not ship outside the city.

What it costs

Delivery is free on orders over $99. Below that, the delivery fee is shown at checkout before you pay.

How long it takes

Orders are prepared within one business day and delivered within two to five business days. We email you when your order is on its way. Large systems that include installation are scheduled with you directly, and the date depends on availability.

Installation

Where installation is included or booked with your order, we contact you to agree a time. Someone over 18 needs to be home, and the technician needs access to the sink or the water line being worked on. If we cannot get access on the day, we will rebook once at no charge.

Returns

You can return an unused system in its original packaging within 30 days of delivery for a full refund of the item price. Start a return by emailing info@crystalina.org with your order number.

Two exceptions, both for hygiene reasons: filter cartridges cannot be returned once the seal is broken, and an installed system cannot be returned, though it is of course still covered by its warranty.

Who pays return delivery

If you changed your mind, you cover the cost of returning the item. If we sent the wrong thing, or it arrived damaged or faulty, we cover it and arrange collection.

Damaged or wrong items

Tell us within 48 hours of delivery and send a photograph if you can. We will replace it or refund you, whichever you prefer, and you will not be out of pocket.

Refunds

Once we have the item back and have checked it, we refund to your original payment method within 10 business days. Delivery charges are refunded only where the return was our fault.

Cancelling an order

Email us before the order ships and we will cancel it and refund you in full. After it ships, it becomes a return.
