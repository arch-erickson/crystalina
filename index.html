Know Your Water

NYC Water Is Famous. It's Also Complicated.

The city's supply is well-treated at the source — the risks come from the journey to your faucet.

125 mi

The journey to your tap. NYC water travels from upstate reservoirs through a century-old network of aqueducts and mains — some laid before 1900.

~130K

Estimated lead service lines still connecting NYC buildings to the water main. If your building is pre-1961, lead solder in the plumbing is also a concern.

1 of 5

NYC is one of only five large U.S. cities with mostly unfiltered surface water — treated with chlorine and UV, which is why your tap can taste and smell like a pool.

100+ yrs

The age of much of the city's water infrastructure. Water main breaks send rust, sediment, and turbidity into building lines hundreds of times each year.
