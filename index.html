Water Facts

Good water is treated at the source. The last mile is yours.

The city does its part well. The pipes between the main and your tap do theirs, and that is where the difference is made.

125 mi

The distance your water travels from upstate reservoirs, through aqueducts and mains laid across more than a century.

~130K

Lead service lines still in use across the city. In buildings from before 1961, the solder in the walls is part of the same story.

1 of 5

One of five large American cities whose surface water goes largely unfiltered, relying on chlorine and UV, which is what you taste.

100+ yrs

The age of much of the network. Every main break sends rust and sediment down the line toward the buildings it serves.
