About Crystalina

Clean water at home, from people who know New York water.

A small New York company. We choose and fit filtration systems for the water that arrives in this city's taps, and we drink it too.

Why we exist

It began with a glass of rust-tinted water.

Crystalina started in an old building after a main break, when the water from the tap was not something anyone wanted to drink. Our founder went looking for a system made for what this city delivers: lead-era plumbing, chlorine you can taste, and apartments where the pipes cannot be touched.

It did not exist, so we built the range and put our name on it. Every system here is chosen and tuned for what arrives in a New York tap, and fitted by our own team when you ask.
